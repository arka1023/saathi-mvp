      How the agents fit together

      InputText, Hinglish, dictation, reel screenshot
      OrchestratorPlans the steps, asks at most one question
      Intent agentThe LLM turns the brief into hard and soft constraints
      Vision agentA vision model reads garment, colour, fabric, style; colour extraction as backup
      Context agentCity climate, occasion norms, day or night
      Fit agentMini You vs each brand’s garment chart
      RetrievalScores all 100 SKUs; hard filters first
      Stylist criticGroups look-alikes, picks 3–4 distinct slots, writes reasons from catalog facts
      Learning loopReturn reasons update your Mini You

      Every reason shown to the shopper is built from catalog fields, so Saathi cannot invent a fabric or a size.

      8-week pilot plan

      1. Weeks 1–2DataTag top 2,000 SKUs (fabric, occasion, pairing); add garment measurements per size per brand.
      2. Weeks 3–4BuildConcierge, Reel-to-Cart and Mini You lite on LLM and vision APIs; fit check at checkout.
      3. Weeks 5–7A/B test10% of search traffic, women’s ethnic and co-ords. Daily guardrails on latency and refusals.
      4. Week 8DecideScale if search-to-checkout ≥ +15% and size/style returns ≤ −10%; else iterate one more cycle.
